About

I taught ICT and Computer Science for twenty years, twelve of them as a head of department. In 2018 I joined a nine-person B2B software company as its support engineer, moved into business development, and then took on information security. I built the company's ISMS from nothing, took it to an ISO 27001:2022 Stage 2 audit in five months, and then ran it.

Since then I have worked on AI governance: risk assessments for AI deployments, vendor evaluation for third-party AI APIs, and a mapping of ISO/IEC 42001 against the NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act. I build small tools with the same systems I assess, which is how I know what they actually do.
